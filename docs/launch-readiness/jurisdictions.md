# Jurisdiction checklists and official source ledger

Sources checked **2026-11-18**. Instrument numbers and years identify the text reviewed. “Verified” means an official source or an official indexed text confirms the instrument, service or stated rule. “Open” means current consolidated wording, effective dates where relevant, applicability, and the proposed platform’s obligations require qualified local advice. No city has a clearance. Entity location, target market, activity, language, placements and actual processing flows can change the analysis. Counsel should confirm authoritative Arabic texts, amendments and regulator responsibilities before relying on translations or portal summaries.

## Evidence required from each country reviewer

Record authoritative instrument and amendment links, effective dates, reviewer and signed date, entity and activity scope, exact permitted categories and features, licence/permit references, language and disclosure copy, moderation and escalation contacts, and data flow and transfer findings. Ask the competent authority for written confirmation where counsel identifies ambiguity. Recheck current regulatory status immediately before any separately approved release. These checklists do not grant legal clearance.
