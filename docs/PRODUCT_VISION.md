# Three-City Directory — Product Vision v1.0

## Mission
Help people discover legitimate local businesses, professionals, services and experiences in Dubai (UAE), Muscat (Oman), and Doha (Qatar) through trustworthy, city-specific discovery. Offer compliant advertising opportunities only after separate authorization.

## Product positioning
A multi-city **general business directory**, not an escort directory, booking platform, marketplace, payment processor, or social network. One shared platform; each city has its own landing page, categories, language/localization configuration, and moderation rules.

## Users and jobs
- Visitors: choose a city, browse/search/filter, evaluate transparent business profiles, report problematic content.
- Business owners (future): claim or submit a business, manage accurate details, receive review decisions.
- Advertisers (future): understand clearly labeled sponsored inventory, submit creatives for review, see aggregate campaign performance.
- Moderators (future): review submissions and reports, document decisions and appeals.
- Administrators (future): manage city/category policy, roles, audit events and feature flags.

## Principles
1. City choice is explicit, never inferred from location or IP.
2. Organic search and sponsored placement are clearly distinguishable.
3. Unverified content is not presented as verified.
4. Privacy by default; collect only necessary data.
5. Accessibility and mobile usability are release criteria.
6. Legal and content-policy review precedes any real listings or commercial launch.
7. No paid services, deployment, external credentials, payment activation, or live onboarding without product-owner approval.

## Success metrics (definitions, not targets)
- Visitor: successful city selection, search success rate, zero-results rate, category engagement.
- Quality: report resolution time, moderation error/appeal rate, stale listing rate.
- Business: verified listing completeness and owner update success.
- Advertising: clearly labeled sponsored impression/click metrics (only when enabled, privacy-reviewed).

## Non-goals for initial phases
No live directory content, no booking, no direct messaging, no checkout, no cryptocurrency payments, no scraped listings, no automated publication, no public launch.
