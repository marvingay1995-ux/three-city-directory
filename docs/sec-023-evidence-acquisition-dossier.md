## 3. Precise questions for a future Oman legal reviewer

1. Given the owner's *actual verified* nationality, immigration/work status, intended role and legal-form choice, can this person lawfully own, manage and work for the proposed business in Oman? What permissions are separate?
2. Which current activity codes and additional approvals apply to an online directory selling sponsored advertisements? Would an activity code be insufficient because of the **specific advertised content**?
3. Is publication or promotion of escort, compensated companionship or sexually suggestive profiles lawful in Oman? Analyze the exact proposed conduct, text, images, contact links, lead routing and advertiser arrangements rather than generic website labels.
4. Does the proposed model create legal exposure for the operator even when the platform claims it only sells advertising and does not arrange meetings or handle service payments?
5. Which restrictions apply to user-generated content, identity and age checks, moderation, complaints, personal data, advertising claims and tax?
6. Could advertising fees be accepted through ordinary banking or cryptocurrency, and what additional payment-provider, financial, AML/CFT, tax and recordkeeping rules apply? If the underlying content is prohibited, confirm that changing payment rails does not make it permissible.
7. Does an Oman-based operator displaying Dubai or Doha content trigger independent legal obligations or restrictions there?
8. What written determinations, licences, documents and review steps are required **before** a release decision can be reconsidered?
9. If the current concept is not lawful, what categories of **independently lawful, non-sexual** digital advertising could be evaluated as a genuinely separate proposal, without implying automatic approval?
