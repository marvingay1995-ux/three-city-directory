# SEC-018 — Candidate registration structures, not an eligibility ruling

**OWNER FACT:** an individual planning to establish a business in Oman, **registration not confirmed**. This fact is insufficient to determine nationality, investment eligibility, actual presence, the legal form of the future business or the advertising permissions it could acquire.

The [Ministry investment-process overview](https://tejarah.gov.om/en/media/news/kapdeds6capkqqvm4ufoayp1) describes various ownership and formation paths and says the legal form changes ownership, capital and licence needs. It suggests sole-proprietorship eligibility may depend on Omani nationality and that foreign ownership varies by sector. The **current consolidated law, eligibility and exact permitted activity must be established independently** for the actual owner, not inferred from a general ministry explainer. No fee/time/foreign-ownership percentage is asserted as a guaranteed outcome.

## Required facts before recommending a path

- Proposed individual's actual eligibility for the specific business form under current Oman rules, without putting nationality, visa scans, IDs or sensitive documents into GitHub.
- Whether a company has already been established, and if not, whether the planned application would be individual-trader, one-person company or LLC; **none presumed**.
- Exact intended activity: site publisher, directory operator, digital advertising seller, marketing/promotions agency or combinations.
- Public target geography, planned ownership/signatory and ongoing management responsibilities; actual establishment address and authority if independently verified.
- Does the marketing/promotion website licence apply to this applicant form? What other commercial, media, electronic commerce, municipal or sector permits apply?
- Confirm official classification/activity code(s), allowed scope, prohibitions, fees and prerequisites only after exact factual and professional review.

## Decision rule

**Do not recommend or register RP-01, RP-02, RP-03 or RP-04 based solely on the owner's statement of future intent.** A lawful entity form and registered commercial activity do not by themselves clear the proposed paid advertising business or any G1–G6 release gate.
