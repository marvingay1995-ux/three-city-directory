# SEC-018 — Activity classification dependencies (no codes assigned)

**CLASSIFICATION STATUS: OPEN.** A general-business directory, advertising seller, marketing company and media publisher may have overlapping but distinct obligations. Do not assign activity codes, declare an exemption or assume that general commercial registration alone authorizes paid advertising.

## Official regulatory leads, not permission

- [Gov.om website/social-media marketing and promotion licence](https://gov.om/en/w/get-a-license-to-practice-marketing-and-promotion-on-social-media-platforms): states that commercial companies can obtain a licence for marketing/promotion on platforms and websites. **Applicability to a particular paid directory, natural person or planned entity is UNKNOWN.**
- [Oman Ministry of Justice and Legal Affairs — Ministerial Decision 619/2022](https://mjla.gov.om/decisions/ar/1088/show/1647): regulation of website/social-media marketing and promotion. Obtain current consolidated Arabic text and amendments for an actual legal opinion.
- [Oman Business Platform via Ministry service directory](https://www.tejarah.gov.om/en/service-directory): establishment and activity licensing access; a portal listing does not constitute a grant.
- Existing [Oman legal/privacy intake](../sec-010/oman-counsel-questions.md) and [jurisdiction ledger](../../launch-readiness/jurisdictions.md) identify additional media, consumer, privacy and intellectual-property questions without resolving them.

## Category boundaries

CAT-01–CAT-06 (interior design, furnishings, non-specialist maintenance, print/graphic studios, non-credentialed workshops and non-medical cultural venues) are **research candidates only — NOT LEGALLY CLEARED**. The present PRD excludes sexual-service/escort ads, exploitation, misleading advertising, unlawful goods/services and unauthorized personal data. Financial, medical, real-estate brokerage, employment/visa and other regulated categories remain outside this phase pending separate review. **No real advertiser may be accepted.**

## Stop and handoff

Do not submit any business or marketing licence application, request codes through a business account, engage outside counsel, sign contracts or activate ads under SEC-018. A future authorized reviewer must provide source-backed codes, entity eligibility, necessary permits, category-by-category restrictions, conditions, renewal/expiry, and an actual signed opinion. No negative finding should be silently converted into an exemption.
