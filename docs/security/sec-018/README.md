## Verified, limited findings

The [Ministry service directory](https://www.tejarah.gov.om/en/service-directory) identifies the Oman Business Platform as a route for business establishment, activity licensing and management. Government services separately describe [individual trader](https://gov.om/w/create-commercial-register-for-individual-trader), [one-person company](https://gov.om/w/apply-for-commercial-registration-for-one-person-company) and [LLC](https://gov.om/w/create-new-commercial-register-for-limited-liability-company) registration.

A [Gov.om marketing and promotion licence service](https://gov.om/en/w/get-a-license-to-practice-marketing-and-promotion-on-social-media-platforms) describes **commercial companies** seeking a licence for marketing/promotion on websites and social platforms. [Ministerial Decision 619/2022](https://mjla.gov.om/decisions/ar/1088/show/1647) is the official regulation lead. Neither web page determines that the owner's unregistered proposal qualifies, or that this licence alone covers a paid directory, media publication, all advertising content or other activities.
