# Separate owner authorization checkpoints

Each decision must identify its exact evidence, approved scope and version, decision owner, date, stop authority, expiry and reopening triggers. Absence of written authority means stop. The current package authorizes internal preparation only.

| Stage | Prerequisites and required exit | Stop boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Market scope and legal work | Owner records whether to study Muscat first. Separately authorize any counsel/Privacy work. Obtain applicable signed operator, licensing and flow opinions before choosing locations or approving processing. | No counsel contact, spending, supplier, real data or market launch from this packet. |
| 2. Independent design review | Owner separately approves exact candidate, named qualified conflict-screened reviewer, data/files, scope, contract and budget as applicable, and permitted offline retests. Obtain written design disposition and reconcile D7 input from AppSec/IAM and Data with counsel/Privacy constraints. | No production access, external testing or engagement before written authorization. |
| 3. Architecture adoption | Owner decides after scoped D7 decisions, independent design review and applicable signatories. Document any deferrals and stop for unresolved prerequisites. | No implied vendor or implementation approval. SEC-007/v1 remains unsigned and unadopted in SEC-010. |
| 4. Synthetic proof | Separately approve isolated test-only scope, fixtures, contract assumptions, acceptance and stop triggers. | No public imports, external targets, real data, external identity or durable infrastructure based on this authorization. |
| 5. Private implementation | Separately approve exact design, approved flows, candidate surfaces, access, environment, infrastructure and test scope after synthetic evidence. | No identity, database, APIs or vendor selection now. Stop on scope or flow changes or missing approval. |
| 6. Independent G2 assessment | Separately approve testing of the exact implementation. Require proof of real IAM and tenant isolation, authority ordering, atomic commit and retry, custody, incident/recovery, mitigation and retests. | A clean audit, passing fixtures or signed design opinion cannot clear G2. |
| 7. Potential pilot decision | Require scoped G1 legal, G2 security, G3 privacy, G4 accessibility, G5 operations and G6 product authorization. Document contingency, monitoring and stop/rollback power. | No pilot, launch or market expansion under SEC-010. Reopen after expiry, material change or incident. |
