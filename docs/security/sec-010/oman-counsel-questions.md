# Oman counsel and Privacy intake

**UNKNOWN / COUNSEL REQUIRED for every item.** Questions below are for qualified Oman counsel and Privacy. Record factual inputs, effective instruments and applicability, signed findings, unresolved exceptions or filings, scope and review date before proposing clearance.

| Topic | Facts to gather and questions for written determination |
| --- | --- |
| Operator, activity and registration | Who operates the service and contracts with users or processors? Where is the entity established? Is the proposed read-only fictional directory classed differently from a directory with real listings, ads or commerce? Which activity codes, registrations, licences, permits or exemptions apply? |
| Content, ads and permitted categories | What restrictions, pre-approvals, language/disclosure or recordkeeping apply to categories, public claims and promotional examples now? What distinct licences, activities and sector restrictions would a later advertising proposal trigger? Keep ads and unresolved categories excluded. |
| Personal-data scope and roles | Inventory public and private fields, technical logs, identity and moderator evidence by purpose, source, necessity and sensitivity. Who acts as controller or processor? What lawful basis, notices, rights and permit triggers apply to each approved purpose? Review demo request logs separately from future protected features. |
| Residency, access and transfers | Map primary, cache, queue, archive, key and backup locations plus restore destinations, remote staff and vendors. Which locations and accesses are permitted, what transfer basis/safeguards and approvals are required, and which flows are prohibited? City authorization establishes no residency. |
| Contracts and processor terms | What terms are required for subprocessors, access, security, breach assistance, audits, data return/deletion, and cross-border access or support? Document requirements without vendor selection. |
| Retention, holds and rights | What schedule and lawful hold rules apply to each class? How must redaction, erasure, audit retention, backup expiry and deletion tombstones work? What notice, intake and response obligations apply? |
| Incidents, complaints and appeals | Which competent authorities and notification, language, escalation or evidence rules apply? How should the operator handle misleading content, verification disputes, takedowns and appeals? Identify accountable roles and independently test proposed procedures after approval. |

Request a signed classification and licensing opinion for G1 and a signed, field-level processing/transfer review for G3 and D7-08/09. For each flow specify operator, data class, purpose, controller/processor, access role, origin and destination, backup/restore, authority and retention. Reassess on a change in operator, features, data or locations. Block unapproved handling. See the [Phase 6 Oman source ledger](../../launch-readiness/jurisdictions.md) for official-source leads. No legal advice, contact or approval is recorded here.
