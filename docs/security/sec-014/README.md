# SEC-014 — Sponsored Advertising Directory Evaluation

**Owner planning direction received 2026-11-21:** “Proceed with SEC-014. My preferred business model for evaluation is Sponsored advertising directory. This is a planning decision only, not authorization for launch or spending.”

**Recorded decision: SELECTED FOR INTERNAL EVALUATION ONLY — sponsored advertising directory (SEC-013 option S).** This is not adoption of a live commercial model, legal activity classification, operator identity, budget or advertising activation.

## Scope and status

- **Research focus:** a hypothetical Muscat-first, three-city capable, general-business directory with clearly disclosed sponsored placements alongside separately ranked organic discovery. Dubai and Doha stay fictional in the present prototype; Muscat-first sequencing is *proposed*, with the prior [SEC-012 scope approval still pending](../sec-012/scope-decision.md).
- **Existing build:** only the fictional private demo; synthetic sponsor examples are demonstrative, not campaigns or inventory for sale.
- **Operator:** UNKNOWN. No company registration, activity code, licences, legal representative, banking, tax or advertising eligibility is assumed.
- **Commercial activity:** NONE APPROVED. No pricing, invoicing, campaign delivery, ad server, advertiser intake, analytics, user/advertiser data, tracking, production hosting, payments, contact/booking or outside engagement.
- **Policy:** preserve the current [general-business PRD](../../PRD.md). No sexual-service/escort advertising, impersonation, trafficking-related content, minors in adult contexts, unapproved regulated categories or unlawful services. This project direction is not amended by selecting an advertising hypothesis.
- **Release:** D-001 NO-GO for Muscat, Dubai and Doha; G1–G6 OPEN, G2 NOT CLEARED; SEC-007/v1 frozen unsigned, D7-08 and D7-12 blocked.

## Evaluation questions

1. Which exact operator and roles (publisher, advertising seller, agency, hosted directory) would this model create? **Unknown until owner facts and qualified country counsel review.**
2. Which advertiser and business categories, placements and disclosures would be eligible? **Unknown; no assumption that any sector is allowed in Oman.**
3. How would sponsored placement remain unmistakably distinct from organic results, never imply endorsement or paid verification, and permit truthful corrections/removal?
4. What independent ad creative review, complaint handling, rights checks, conflict safeguards and advertiser identity proofs would have to exist before publication?
5. What cost, demand, sales-cycle, price, renewal and refund assumptions are needed to make an honest financial feasibility model? **No data verified.**
6. What log, event and campaign reporting data could be minimized or avoided, and what privacy/transfer/data residency decisions are needed before any selection of vendors?
7. What would qualified Oman counsel determine about operator registration, advertising/media/marketing permissions, regulated categories, consumer disclosures, language and taxation? **No opinion obtained here.**
