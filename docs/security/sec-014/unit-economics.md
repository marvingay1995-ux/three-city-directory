# SEC-014 — Advertising economics and viability hypotheses

**No prices, payer counts, conversion rates, ad impressions, budgets, audience demand or competitor revenue have been verified.** These variables describe a model to test when separate market research/engagement is authorized. Do not confuse a variable with a target or claim of viability.

## Proposed pricing mechanisms (not selected)

| Model | Potential billable unit | Primary unknowns and risks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed city-card sponsorship | Eligible placement per defined period | Placement demand, approved inventory, terms, fairness and renewal risk |
| Fixed category sponsorship | Eligible category/period | Audience relevance and lawful category restrictions; small inventory |
| Sponsored editorial feature | One disclosed reviewed feature/period | Content production, rights, moderation burden and disclosure compliance |
| CPM / CPC | Measured impressions or clicks | Requires reliable, lawfully authorized, privacy-reviewed measurement, disputes and anti-fraud controls; **not approved** |

Early research comparison should prefer the simplest transparent fixed-period proposal *as a hypothesis* because it requires fewer measurement assumptions than impression/click billing; this is not advice that such charging is lawful or viable.

## Equations for a later evidence-backed scenario

`gross_period_revenue = min(N, I) * F`
`net_period_revenue = gross_period_revenue - R`
`period_contribution = net_period_revenue - N * (C_review + C_var) - C_acq`
`period_result_before_tax = period_contribution - C_fixed`

Do **not** infer N from visitors, extrapolate F from competitors without evidence, or manufacture break-even. No forecasting output or scenario can be called validated until input sources, period definitions, lawful sellable inventory and repeatable calculations are checked. Avoid treating promotional exposure as guaranteed results.

## Go/no-go criteria for future commercial hypothesis

- Explicit exact operator/activity and eligible categories, supported by qualified Oman counsel.
- Demonstrated lawful product and platform terms, real buyer interest gathered after separate authorization.
- Transparent sponsored/organic distinction and verified ad claims/rights.
- Testable operations workload, complaint/appeals costs, realistic cost ceiling and staff coverage.
- Data and tracking plan reviewed by Privacy; any billing/measurement additional constraints decided separately.
- Owner approval of commercial model, spend and eventual release with all G1–G6 separately satisfied.

**SEC-014 evaluates only: it does not authorize pricing offers, advertiser contact, sales, ad hosting, payment or spend.**
