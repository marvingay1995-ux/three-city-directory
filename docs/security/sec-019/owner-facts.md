## Confidential evidence rules

**Do not commit** passports, residency/visa documents, nationalities, birthdays, personal addresses, financial accounts, application credentials, owner signatures, private contacts or registration scans to this repository. If later authorized, supply necessary eligibility evidence directly through a mutually approved confidential professional channel; record only non-sensitive outcome, scope, decision date, source and expiry in the repo.

An owner's preference for a form is a **research hypothesis only**. Do not convert it to verified eligibility, licence, business registration, lawful commercial activity or permission to hire counsel. Missing facts remain UNKNOWN or PENDING.
