## Required comparisons before recommending any form

1. Actual proposed legal owner profile, **only in private authorized channels**; for GitHub record eligibility as UNKNOWN or reviewer-verified outcome without citizenship, visa, address, passport or other private documents.
2. Whether a company exists; as of the owner's statement **registration is not confirmed**, which must never be restated as definitive absence.
3. Precise business activities: editorial directory publisher, company selling own sponsored website inventory, marketing/promotion service to third parties, production of creatives, or hybrid; they may have different permissions.
4. Whether a form is available for this owner and exact activities under *current* Omani rules, including any sector and foreign investment limitations.
5. Whether applicable advertising/website/media licensing requires a **commercial company** or additional separate permissions; distinguish business registration from marketing licence, media/publisher rules, and regulated advertiser categories.
6. Tax/accounting/consumer obligations, staff/representative requirements, fees and recurrence, but **no fee commitment or incorporation action** in this phase.

## Relative research priority, not legal recommendation

RP-02 and RP-03 are reasonable **comparison scenarios** for a prospective company selling sponsored placements because the official marketing/promotion service describes *commercial companies*. That wording does **not** establish eligibility for either structure, and it does **not** eliminate RP-01 or require incorporation. RP-01 must be tested against the actual applicant and promotion rules; RP-04 stays deferred absent a foreign parent. No default RP-02 selection is implied. Any formal recommendation requires an identified applicant and independent current classification.
