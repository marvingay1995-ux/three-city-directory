## Facts requiring independent legal/Privacy conclusions

Questions, not claims: which Oman business activity codes and registration/licence requirements govern a read-only directory; whether any website/media/marketing authorization applies to editorial presentation or sponsored placement; whether proposed categories trigger restrictions or other sector permissions; what consumer language/disclosure and complaint duties would attach; which data-processing, notices, controller/processor roles, transfers, incident reports and retention rules apply to the actual operator and hosting flow.

Ground a future counsel assignment in the existing [Oman intake](../sec-010/oman-counsel-questions.md) and official-source [jurisdiction ledger](../../launch-readiness/jurisdictions.md). Counsel must verify current consolidated texts, Arabic originals where relevant, implementation, exemptions and effective dates; the present register confers **no legal advice or clearance**.

## Dependency order

- First: owner records M-01 through M-08, including excluded operations.
- Second: separately authorize qualified counsel/Privacy to evaluate the exact scope and M-09 for permissible candidate flows.
- Third: obtain signed and dated applicability outcomes, registration/activity/sector determinations and any required permissions before advertising, provider onboarding, vendor commitments or public hosting are considered.
- Fourth: separately seek independent security, accessibility and operational assessments and G6 release approval only for an exact implementation and permitted market.

If identity, categories, ad model, target country, data flows, audience or external providers change, reopen this register, D7-08 and affected G1–G6 decisions. Keep status **NO-GO** pending independent evidence.
