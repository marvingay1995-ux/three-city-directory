## Model choices for later owner decision

| Candidate | How value would be delivered | Possible revenue hypothesis (not authorized) | Principal dependencies | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **O — Editorial / noncommercial** | Curate genuinely useful public information about eligible businesses with permission/rights verification when real content is separately authorized | None; owner-funded operating costs | Operator classification, content rights, privacy of logs, accessibility, complaint response | **PROPOSED FOR RESEARCH ONLY** |
| **L — Paid directory listings** | Businesses pay a subscription/flat fee for a clearly described listing product; ranking integrity and claim verification must be defined | Subscription / listing fees | Real business onboarding, activity registration, identity verification, tax, refunds, consumer disclosure, security, G1–G6 | **NOT APPROVED** |
| **S — Sponsored advertising** | Advertisers buy distinctly labeled placement, separated from organic results | Placement/campaign fees | Advertising/marketing/media classification, creative review, advertiser eligibility, invoicing, rights, privacy-safe measurement | **NOT APPROVED** |
| **R — Referral/lead introduction** | Facilitate consumer inquiries to businesses | Referral fee / qualified lead | Personal-data sharing, consent, service classification, complaints, deceptive lead risks; no contacts/introductions in existing scope | **OUT OF CURRENT SCOPE** |
| **T — Transaction/booking marketplace** | Arrange appointments/orders and payments | Transaction commission | Much greater legal, fraud, payment, insurance, consumer and data-security footprint | **OUT OF CURRENT SCOPE** |

None is confirmed as legal, licensed, profitable, desirable or operationally feasible in Oman. SEC-013 does **not** select O as a live business. O is simply the narrowest scope to analyze first without making pricing, tracking, ad-serving or contact commitments.
