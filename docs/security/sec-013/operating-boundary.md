# SEC-013 — Service, data and operations boundaries

## Existing approved prototype
A private, offline, synthetic **general-business** design for Dubai, Muscat and Doha. Features: city navigation; search, sorting, category filters; fictional listing pages and artwork galleries; session-only comparison; fictional sponsorship labels; noindex metadata. UX-010 explicitly approved visual design **only**.

## Future operating capabilities: NOT AUTHORIZED
- **Business identity and registration:** validation, representatives, claims, proof of rights, duplicate controls, access provisioning.
- **Editorial publishing:** real descriptions, locations, photos, amendments, sponsor review, reporting and appeal system.
- **Advertising:** contracts, pricing, campaign delivery, measurement, creative eligibility and revenue settlement.
- **Customer communications:** lead capture, messaging, contact routing, bookings, direct introductions or payments.
- **Production assurance:** IAM, cross-city/tenant isolation, backups and restoration, key control, audit custody, incident/abuse response, analytics and data minimization.
- **Hosting:** public or private preview, cloud vendor selection, regional routing, CDN, external services and personal-data processing.

## Change controls
Reopen operator, counsel, privacy and product assessment whenever jurisdiction, operator, paid model, real content category, age/audience, contact/booking capabilities, identity fields, processing/backup locations, suppliers, staffing or intended publication changes. A generic “next”, a green CI run or a document merge cannot approve commercial features.
