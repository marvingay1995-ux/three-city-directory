# SEC-013 — Business-model options and feasibility worksheet

**All revenue models below are hypotheses, not commitments, price quotes, authorizations, market demand findings or legal determinations.** Current platform is a fictional offline editorial prototype; it generates **zero live business revenue**. No new permissions arise from these comparisons.

## Demand and value hypotheses to test later
- **Visitors:** useful city-scoped discovery and accurate, clearly labeled business profiles might reduce search friction. Demand is **unmeasured**.
- **Eligible business operators:** an approved listing product might offer discoverability, but interest and willingness to pay are **unmeasured**.
- **Advertisers:** a labeled placement could create value only if the operator, sector, media permissions and rights are verified, inventory is measurable in a privacy-reviewed way, and actual buyers are identified; **all unknown**.
- **Moderation and public trust:** truthful claims, right of correction, reporting, safeguarding and complaint resolution are costs and obligations, not optional marketing features.

For a later **approved** subscription model only, compute:
`net_monthly_revenue = active_payers × realized_monthly_fee`;
`contribution_before_tax = net_monthly_revenue − variable_service_costs − payment_fees − refunds`;
`operating_result = contribution_before_tax − fixed_operations − compliance − infrastructure − acquisition_spend`.
Do not calculate a break-even count without meaningful confirmed costs, market demand and fees. The present data cannot support a profit forecast.

## Required evidence before selecting paid model
1. Owner selects exactly **which** legal operator and commercial activities to investigate.
2. Qualified Oman counsel confirms applicability, permitted sectors and permits for the exact candidate; Privacy signs a scoped data-flow basis.
3. Owner approves any paid market research, customer interviews, vendor quotation, external contact and spending **separately**.
4. Define categories, fairness and disclosure controls; verify commercial documents and secure business account/identity requirements before any real feature.
5. Only after independent G1–G5 and explicit G6 authorization consider an actual launch. No ads, onboarding, payments, pricing display or tracking activation now.

**Working sequence suggestion:** compare O and L on paper first; only research S, R or T after a deliberate owner product decision. This is an option ranking for research burden, **not** a legal ranking or operational recommendation.
