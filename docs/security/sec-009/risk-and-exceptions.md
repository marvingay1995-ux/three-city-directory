## Exception control

Record the ID, affected requirements and exact candidate/environment/market scope, risk rationale, denied alternatives, mitigation evidence, accountable owner and monitoring/stop criteria. Obtain independent disposition and any required Privacy/counsel concurrence before the owner can issue a dated, scoped decision. Record expiry, independent retest date and result. Reopen upon incident, changed scope, expired evidence or failed control, and hold affected access when required evidence is unavailable.

Unauthorized writes/replays, non-atomic audited commits, cross-city private leakage and unapproved data transfers cannot be waived. An exception cannot silently revise SEC-007/v1, clear a release gate or authorize a deployment.
