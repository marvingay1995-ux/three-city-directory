# SEC-015 — Proposed advertising categories and exclusion register

**These are hypotheses for internal screening, not permitted-sector findings. No real ads or businesses can be admitted from this table.** A qualified Oman reviewer must establish the relevant classification, sector permissions and disclosure requirements for the exact operator and use case. Dubai/UAE and Doha/Qatar require separate independent reviews.

## A. Candidate general-business categories — STUDY ONLY / NOT CLEARED

| ID | Example intended advertiser category | Initial product rationale | Required checks before any real candidate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAT-01 | Interior design and residential decor studios | Local discovery matching design-oriented directory | Registered operator, truthful services, design/media rights and any licensing restrictions |
| CAT-02 | Furniture, lighting and home furnishings retailers | Tangible home products relevant to the audience | Business identity, truthful product claims, product standards if applicable, photo rights |
| CAT-03 | General repair and non-specialist home maintenance | Local service discovery | Scope of trade, permits/qualifications, safety claims, consumer complaint route |
| CAT-04 | Print, graphic design and creative production studios | Transparent B2B services with illustrative portfolios | Copyright/trademark use, identity, authorized samples |
| CAT-05 | General education workshops (non-credentialed, non-regulated topics) | Local hobby and creative interest discovery | Whether claimed courses/credentials trigger education or other permission |
| CAT-06 | Non-medical recreational and cultural events/venues | Local places and experiences | Venue/event permissions, accessibility and promotional rights; no sale of tickets under this scope |

**Category status for all CAT-01–06: RESEARCH CANDIDATE — NOT LEGALLY CLEARED.** The descriptors expressly avoid claims that a business is legitimate, verified, licensed or safe without evidence.

## B. Categories requiring separate sector-specific classification — EXCLUDED FOR NOW

- Healthcare, wellness treatment or medical claims; pharmacies, prescription products and medical devices.
- Financial, investment, insurance, securities, crypto trading and related promotions.
- Real estate brokerage and property sales/rentals where specific operator or advertiser permissions may apply.
- Employment, immigration, visas, staffing and recruitment.
- Food/alcohol, age-restricted goods, transport services and regulated tourism services.
- Any other sector involving separate licensing, sensitive personal data, vulnerable groups or unverified credentials.

This is a **non-exhaustive caution list**, not a legal conclusion that any category is categorically banned in Oman.

## C. Current product prohibitions — NOT CANDIDATES

Advertising for sexual services or escort arrangements; trafficking-related material; exploitation, minors in adult contexts; unlawful goods/services; impersonation or deceptive claims; unauthorized personal data; illicit drugs and unapproved regulated offerings. No attempt to disguise the activity through coded descriptions, hidden links or indirect Telegram/WhatsApp contacts. The source PRD's content rules remain effective.

## Eligibility pipeline required in a future separately authorized system (NOT BUILT)

1. Establish exact operator, location, service activity and sponsor-category classification.
2. Verify applicable advertiser entity, representative, sector authorizations and content rights.
3. Review every creative version, factual claim, disclosure and destination independently; fail closed on ambiguity.
4. Maintain prominent text **Sponsored** labeling distinct from organic relevance and ensure city/category scope.
5. Record decision, expiry, complaint/takedown/appeal and evidence handling under approved data/retention policy.
6. Suspend on licence loss, unresolved complaint, misleading claims, category change or owner stop.

No production moderation, identity checking, billing, consumer contact routing or tracking is activated by SEC-015.

## Owner category decision — PENDING

The proposed candidate list is a **draft shortlist**, not owner approval of individual categories. Owner may accept the list *for internal study*, revise items, or defer. Qualified local applicability determination remains mandatory even if the owner selects categories for evaluation.
