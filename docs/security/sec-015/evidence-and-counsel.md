# SEC-015 — Evidence needed before qualified Oman review

This checklist prepares **an unsent internal review package**. It is not legal advice and does not authorize contacting counsel, regulator, providers or advertisers. Obtain current authoritative local instruments and applicability analysis only under a separate engagement authorization.

| Subject | Missing fact or question | Connected gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operator identity | Which exact person/entity, registration, authorised representative, branch/foreign presence, business activity and contract role? | G1 / G6 |
| Website/marketing/media | Does operating a digital general-business directory with paid placements require business, website, promotion, media or advertising permission? Which authority, fees and scope? | G1 |
| Advertiser classes | Which CAT-01–06 classes may be proposed and what business/sector documents and claims/disclosure are required? | G1 / G5 |
| Sponsored vs organic ranking | What specific sponsorship labeling, advertiser identification, Arabic/other language disclosures, misleading claims and complaints obligations apply? | G1 / G4 / G5 |
| Ownership/rights | Who controls logos, photos, trademarks, customer testimonials, portfolios and withdrawal? | G1 / G5 |
| Data roles | Which operator/ad seller is controller or processor for identities, server logs, campaign evidence or aggregate reporting? | G3 |
| Hosting/transfer | Which countries will store/process logs, backups and credentials? What residency/transfers and support access are permitted? | G3 / D7-08 |
| Security/abuse | Who reviews ads, manages authorization, version changes, city isolation, audit evidence and emergency suspension? | G2 / G5 |
| Finance/consumer terms | Who invoices, collects money, pays taxes, handles refunds, disputes and paid contractual disclosures? | G1 / G5 |
| Operation/release | What staffing, training, incident response, content withdrawal and rollback will be available? | G5 / G6 |

**Evidence required for each potential positive conclusion:** identified qualified reviewer, authoritative legal source, effective date, exact operator, category and country, authorized features, permits or exclusions, signed opinion, unresolved conditions, renewal/expiry and reopening triggers. Do not treat a portal page or an automated test as an operating licence.
