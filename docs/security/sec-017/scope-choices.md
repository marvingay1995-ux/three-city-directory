## Research product definition for future counsel

Possible *future* product, not current functionality: independently screened businesses may seek distinct **Sponsored** placements next to an organic city directory. Paid listings are not misrepresented as organic or independently verified; visible sponsor identity, category/city eligibility, rights checks, manual rejection, expiry, complaint/takedown, disclosure and accessibility requirements require independent review. No impression/click tracking, pricing, invoicing, campaigns, creative uploads, contact routing, bookings or sales are active.

**Present state:** six fictional profiles in private demo, with no production advertiser or user handling. Existing PRD excludes sexual services/escort ads, trafficking/exploitation, minors in adult contexts, deceptive claims, unlawful or unapproved regulated services, and unauthorized personal details.
