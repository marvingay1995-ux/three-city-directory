# Authorization contract — SEC-007/v1

All A7 invariants are FROZEN FOR REVIEW. The synthetic handles in SEC-005 establish test provenance only.

| ID | Preconditions and refusal contract |
| --- | --- |
| A7-01 Trusted identity | Authenticating ingress MUST derive a stable internal actor from a configured trusted issuer and verified subject, audience, integrity and expiry, with nonce/state and PKCE where applicable. A caller actor/role/city/owner claim MUST NOT create authority. Missing, malformed, expired or unprovable identity denies protected access. An ID token MUST NOT be accepted as a general API bearer. |
| A7-02 Session, grant and policy freshness | Protected operations MUST require an active session, current policy and versioned, unexpired city/action grant. Privileged operations require approved phishing-resistant MFA/step-up. Issuance, recovery, role change, revocation and expiry MUST invalidate obsolete authority; recovery MUST NOT increase it. Logout and loss of grants must take effect according to A7-05. Timing and assurance levels remain OPEN under D7-02. |
| A7-03 City, object and ownership | Resolve requested city against the approved fixed registry and resolve records under mandatory city/object predicates. Protected queries and writes MUST require the exact current scoped grant and ownership where applicable, including through repository and service layers. Unknown, mismatched and forbidden private objects MUST yield indistinguishable public refusal classes without resource IDs, state or receipt disclosure. Future timing/existence testing remains required. No wildcard or fallback city. |
| A7-04 Role and duty separation | An owner may submit an owned version. A scoped moderator reviews eligible versions, separate verifier verifies, and distinct authorized publisher publishes. Compare stable participant identities for the exact content version across role changes. Self-approval and self-grant escalation MUST fail. Deny unresolved owner/affiliate conflicts. Auditor receives only authorized case/city audit reads and never gains business-write or custody rights. Affiliate criteria and ownership-transfer approval remain OPEN under D7-05. |
| A7-05 Commit and retry freshness | Admission authority MUST be rechecked at the authoritative commit/read-receipt boundary, using current session, actor, grant, policy, ownership and conflict context. Revocation committed first MUST prevent a write or receipt disclosure; a commit ordered first retains its history, while later replay after loss of authority denies. A short-lived permit, hosted policy call or earlier queue message alone MUST NOT bypass the fence. See T7-02/T7-03. |
| A7-06 Worker, cache and export boundaries | Background principals MUST be distinct, purpose-limited and city/scope constrained. Actor-dependent work MUST reauthorize originating authority at the protected effect. Audit/media/exports require explicit scoped purpose, recipient and minimized output. Private responses MUST NOT enter shared caches. Public projection/cache must be separated by city and honor current visibility under T7-05. Unknown or unauthorized messages are refused or quarantined without raw private payloads. |

Use neutral refusal for missing/forbidden private identities and objects, typed conflict/revision errors only after authorized scope resolution, and generic unavailable results when required authority cannot be established. Error, cache and log content MUST NOT leak privileged state. No production error-code or API route is selected. Production tests must prove both user-facing and server/worker/storage enforcement.
