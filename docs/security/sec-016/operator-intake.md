## Minimum facts before a meaningful counsel opinion

The precise **operator**, business activities, **countries**, permitted-category shortlist, ad/organic boundary, intended customer relationship, log and data roles, and potential hosting/transfer flows must be documented by the owner. Where something remains unknown, counsel can provide only conditional alternatives, **not** a clearance. Ask for a review of classification **before** assuming one register or promotion permit fits the model.

### Handling of proof documents

Never paste passports, visa/residence details, national IDs, bank account details, client files or personally identifying registration extracts into this repository. If the owner later authorizes counsel engagement, identify a confidential transfer route and minimal document set outside this repository, subject to privacy approval.
