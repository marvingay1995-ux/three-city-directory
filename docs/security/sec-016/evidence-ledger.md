# SEC-016 — Internal provenance and evidence limitations

| Input | Grounded repository record | What it does NOT prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current fictional product | [Product Vision](../../PRODUCT_VISION.md), [PRD](../../PRD.md) | Operator registration, sector permissions or public-launch authorization |
| Owner visual decision | [UX-010](../../reviews/UX-010-VISUAL-DESIGN-SIGNOFF.md) | Legal, Privacy, WCAG or production release sign-off |
| Launch blockers | [SEC-011 readiness](../sec-011/pre-release-readiness.md), [release gates](../../launch-readiness/release-gates.md) | Independent resolution of any gate |
| Muscat market study | [SEC-012](../sec-012/README.md), [scope decision](../sec-012/scope-decision.md) | Actual owner approval of research scope or pilot |
| Business model preference | [SEC-014 owner decision](../sec-014/decision.md) | Any implementation/price/ad sale authority |
| Candidate operator and categories | [SEC-015](../sec-015/README.md), [roles](../sec-015/operator-roles.md), [categories](../sec-015/advertising-categories.md) | Verified entity, selected structure or category legal clearance |
| Legal research leads | [Oman counsel intake](../sec-010/oman-counsel-questions.md), [jurisdiction sources](../../launch-readiness/jurisdictions.md) | Current consolidated text verified for this operator or a signed applicability opinion |
| Security contract | [SEC-007/v1](../sec-007/README.md) and [SEC-010 checkpoints](../sec-010/authorization-checkpoints.md) | Adopted architecture, production IAM, independent G2 approval |

**Freshness rule:** before sending a separately authorized external brief, reconcile current official laws, regulator names, publication/effective/amendment dates, actual entity/activities, counsel qualifications and precise data processing. The previously assembled jurisdiction ledger is a dated **lead**, not a live clearance. Do not treat passing unit tests as legal evidence.

**SEC-016 conducted no contact with counsel, authorities or vendors. It does not generate or validate personal identity documents, permits, signed opinions, market-demand estimates or commercial quotes.**
