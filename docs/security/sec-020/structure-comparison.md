## Decision criteria to evaluate, not make assumptions about

1. **Eligibility:** precise form and ownership eligibility, current law and activity restrictions for this individual, checked through a qualified authorized route; no nationality/visa assertions in the repository.
2. **Correct commercial activities:** editorial directory publisher versus seller of its own sponsored inventory versus third-party advertising/creative agency. These may have different registration codes and regulatory obligations.
3. **Additional marketing/media approvals:** Oman government's [website/social promotion licence service](https://gov.om/en/w/get-a-license-to-practice-marketing-and-promotion-on-social-media-platforms) refers to **commercial companies**; this does not prove any route meets its definition or that this is the only required licence.
4. **Liability and governance:** responsible publisher/advertiser seller, sponsored disclosure, contracts, moderation/appeal authority, brand and IP ownership.
5. **Economic and administrative burden:** source-backed formation/renewal fees, recurring accounting/tax and staffing obligations, not invented costs.
6. **Growth and territorial reach:** whether any later UAE/Qatar-facing operation requires independent establishment and additional legal/Privacy clearances.
